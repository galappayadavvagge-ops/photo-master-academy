Aperture Control

Aperture controls the size of the opening inside your lens. A smaller f-number means a wider opening and more light.

f/2.8 · Wide opening · More light · Shallow depth of field
Tip: Move the slider slowly. Try f/2.8, f/5.6 and f/11 and compare the aperture opening.
